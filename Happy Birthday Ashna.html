Meri Moti biyaaa..

Aaj tumhari salgirah hai, aur mere liye yeh din kisi tyohar se kam nahi. Is duniya mein lakhon log hain, lekin mujhe tum mili, aur yeh meri zindagi ki sab se khoobsurat baat hai. Aaj main tumhe woh sab batana chahta hoon jo shayad main roz kehna bhool jata hoon.

Pehli Nazar

Jab maine tumhe pehli baar dekha tha, mujhe nahi pata tha ke yeh chehra meri zindagi ka sab se pyara hissa ban jayega. Tumhari muskurahat mein ek sukoon tha, jaise barish ke baad ki pehli khushbu. Us din se mera dil tumhara ho gaya.

Tumhari Muskurahat

Tumhari hansi mere zindagi ki sab se haseen awaaz hai. Jab tum muskurati ho to thakan, fikar aur har pareshani peeche reh jati hai. Agar mujhe koi ek cheez hamesha ke liye dekhni ho, to wo tumhari muskurahat hogi.

Tumhara Pyar

Tum bina kuch kahe meri har baat samajh jati ho. Meri khamoshi mein bhi tum mera haal parh leti ho. Tumhara pyar shor nahi karta, woh dheere se dil mein utar jata hai aur wahin ghar bana leta hai.

Mera Sukoon

Din bhar ki bhaag daud ke baad jab main ghar aata hoon, to tum meri manzil ho. Tumhare paas baith kar mujhe lagta hai ke saari duniya ruk gayi hai. Tum meri thakan ki dawa ho aur meri raaton ka chain.

Tumhari Qurbaniyan

Mujhe pata hai ke tumne meri khushi ke liye kitni baar apni khwahishon ko peeche rakha. Tumne kabhi jataya nahi, kabhi shikayat nahi ki. Main tumhara shukar guzar hoon, aur dil se kehta hoon ke main tumhari har kurbani ki qadar karta hoon.

Hamari Yaadein

Hamari choti choti baatein, woh hansi mazaaq, woh saath ki chai, woh bina wajah ki batein, yehi meri sab se qeemti doulat hain. Har yaad ek phool ki tarah hai jo mere dil ke guldaste mein hamesha taza rehta hai.

Mushkil Waqt Mein

Jab bhi zindagi ne mujhe aazmaya, tum meri himmat ban kar mere saath khadi rahi. Tumhara ek jumla, ek nazar, meri saari himmat wapas laa deti hai. Tum sirf meri biwi nahi, meri sab se achhi dost bhi ho.

Tumhari Khoobsurti

Tum sirf chehre se hi nahi, apne dil, apne akhlaq aur apni narmi se bhi bohot khoobsurat ho. Tumhari aankhon mein woh chamak hai jo mujhe har din tumse naye sire se pyar karne par majboor kar deti hai.

Mera Wada

Main tumse wada karta hoon ke tumhari muskurahat ki hifazat karunga. Tumhari baat sununga, tumhara khayal rakhunga, aur har mushkil mein tumhara haath thaame rakhunga. Tum meri zimmedari nahi, meri sab se badi khushi ho.

Tumhare Liye Dua

Allah tumhe sehat, khushiyan aur lambi umar ata farmaye. Tumhari har dua qabool ho, tumhare har khwab ko manzil mile. Tumhara har din phoolon ki tarah mehakta rahe, aur tumhara har lamha roshan ho.

Hamara Aane Wala Kal

Main ek aisi zindagi dekhta hoon jahan hum dono saath boorhe hon, purani yaadon par hanste hon, aur ek doosre ka haath thame hon. Tumhare bina us khwab ka koi matlab nahi. Tum hi meri kahani ho aur tum hi uska sab se haseen mod.
